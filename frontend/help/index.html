Asked most often

Can I sign without an account?

No. Signing a document needs an account. Without one you can still open a signing request someone sent you.

Go to signing →
What does it cost?

ParaSign Community is free, forever, and no card is required. It covers 2 signatures a month. The first paid plan is Firm at €29 a month excl. btw (€35.09 incl.), which includes 100 signatures a month and 500 transfers a month. Every plan gets the same encryption, the same post-quantum signatures and the same public proof log.

See pricing →
Where does my data live?

Your documents live on servers at Hetzner Nuremberg, Germany, and they sit there as ciphertext. The relay never sees plaintext and never holds a private key. Your files stay under EU law and the GDPR, and no US provider is in the data path. Email goes out via Resend, as /privacy sets out.
