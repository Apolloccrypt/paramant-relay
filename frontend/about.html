Who builds Paramant

This page states who is behind Paramant, where your data is processed, how to reach us, and the limits we are upfront about.

Who builds this

Paramant is built by Paramantis Solutions B.V., a Dutch company based in Harderwijk, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce 42115132).

Founder: Mick Beer. Alongside Paramant he works as a privacy researcher, investigating how organisations handle personal data.

That background shapes how the product is built. The emphasis on data minimisation and on verifiable, documented claims comes from examining what goes wrong when organisations hold more personal data than they need, or state more than they can show.

Where your data is

Servers: Hetzner, Nuremberg, Germany (network zone eu-central). EU jurisdiction. No US subprocessor for document handling.

Document payloads exist only in server memory on that EU infrastructure and are destroyed on first read. The one flow that leaves the EU is transactional email, for example an account-verification message or a signing invite, sent through a US provider under Standard Contractual Clauses; it never carries file contents.

Both are set out in full in our Data Processing Agreement. Every sub-processor we use, and the country each one operates from, is listed in Section 5 of that agreement.

How to reach us

General and business enquiries: info@paramant.app.

Privacy and data-protection questions: privacy@paramant.app.

What we are honest about

  • The relay is source-available under BUSL-1.1, not open source. The SDK is Apache-2.0.
  • ParaID is in beta.
  • No qualified signatures (eIDAS QES) yet.
