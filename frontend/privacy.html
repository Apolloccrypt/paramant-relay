Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how PARAMANT processes your personal data when you use paramant.app and the PARAMANT services (Ghost Pipe, ParaShare, ParaSign, the relay, and the SDK). It is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG).

The controller responsible for the processing described here is:

  • [PARAMANT legal entity, e.g. PARAMANT B.V.]
  • Registered address: [registered address]
  • Company registration: [KvK / company number]
  • Privacy contact: privacy@paramant.app · controller contact: [controller contact email]

We have not appointed a statutory Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to under Article 37 GDPR. For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights set out below, contact privacy@paramant.app.

For file content you move through the service, the roles are different: you are the controller and PARAMANT acts only as a processor. See Controller and processor roles below.

Core principle

PARAMANT collects only what is strictly required to operate your account and run the service (data minimisation, Article 5(1)(c) GDPR).

An email address is required to create an account and recover access; it is personal data, and it is stored. No phone number. We do not log IP addresses for analytics or profiling; an IP is processed only transiently, for security and abuse-prevention (rate limiting, the session record, an audit log, and the DPA signature record). Only a strictly-necessary session cookie (no tracking cookies). No tracking pixels. No analytics. No advertisements. We never sell your data, and we never use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

How Ghost Pipe & ParaShare work

All files are encrypted client-side in your browser using post-quantum cryptography (ML-KEM-768 + ECDH P-256 + AES-256-GCM + HKDF-SHA256) before they ever reach our servers. The relay never sees plaintext at any point.

Files are split into 5 MB encrypted chunks. Each chunk exists only in RAM on our relay server and is permanently and irreversibly destroyed after the first download (burn-on-read). Encrypted payload data is never written to disk. From a transfer, the only thing persisted to disk is cryptographic hashes in the public Certificate Transparency log: no file content, no keys, no plaintext. (Account data, listed below, is stored separately to run your account.)

Free tier blobs expire after 1 hour maximum. Pro blobs after 24 hours. Enterprise blobs after 7 days. All are destroyed earlier if downloaded.

How long we keep account data

Temporary relay data is governed by the TTLs above and is usually destroyed much sooner on download. Account data follows these retention rules:

  • Email address and account profile: kept while your account exists; deleted within 30 days of you closing the account, except where a legal obligation requires us to keep a limited record longer.
  • Sign-in factors (passkey public keys, encrypted TOTP secret, hashed recovery codes): deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • ParaSign signing public key: removed from your account on closure; the one-way hash already written to the public Certificate Transparency log remains, because it is what makes past signatures verifiable and it cannot be reversed to a person.
  • Billing records (plan, invoices, anonymised payment reference): retained up to [7 years, the NL statutory tax-administration term] after the transaction, to satisfy the legal obligation in Article 6(1)(c); then deleted.
  • Security and audit logs (transient IP / user-agent): kept only for the short session or rate-limit window, or until rotated out by the audit-log volume cap.

What we never do

  • Sell your data, or use it for advertising or profiling
  • Carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects (Article 22 GDPR): we do none
  • Log or store IP addresses for analytics or profiling (an IP is used only transiently, for security and abuse-prevention)
  • Use tracking cookies or browser fingerprinting (only a strictly-necessary session cookie is set after login)
  • Run advertising or tracking scripts
  • Share your data with third parties, except the operational subprocessors needed to run the service (hosting, transactional email, and payments when enabled, listed below)
  • Write file data to disk at any point
  • Load fonts, scripts, styles, or any other resource from a third party. Everything is self-hosted on paramant.app

Cookies

We set one cookie, and only after you log in:

  • paramant_session (name may vary): a strictly-necessary, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite session cookie that keeps you signed in. Purpose: authentication only. Duration: the browser session, or until you sign out or it expires. It carries no tracking identifier and is exempt from consent because it is strictly necessary to deliver the service you asked for.

We set no analytics, advertising, or third-party cookies, so there is no cookie banner to click.

No third-party requests

Every byte your browser loads on paramant.app comes from paramant.app. No web fonts from Google, no JavaScript or CSS from a CDN, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no embedded third-party widgets. Open your browser’s network inspector on any page: every request goes to one origin, and nowhere else.

This is deliberate. A single request to a third party leaks your IP address and the page you are viewing to that party before you have agreed to anything. So we self-host everything: fonts (a plain system font stack, zero font files downloaded), scripts, styles, and images all come from paramant.app.

What runs the service is operational, never tracking: our own edge (Caddy) terminates TLS directly on our server (see below); transactional email is sent server-side via our email provider; and a payment processor will handle billing on its own pages once that is enabled. None of these is loaded into the site, and none follows you across it.

Local storage in your browser

For technical functionality, the following data is stored locally in your browser only. It never leaves your device:

  • ECDH + ML-KEM key pairs: generated locally for end-to-end encryption (SDK / Ghost Pipe sessions)
  • Nonce registry: replay-attack protection (SDK sessions)
  • Free tier usage counter (ps_free_uses): today's date and upload count, used to enforce the 10/day limit client-side
  • Docs access key (pm_docs_key): stored if you authenticate to the Docs with a paid API key
  • Passkey / WebAuthn credentials: your passkey’s private key is created and held by your device or password manager. PARAMANT only ever stores its public key, server-side; the private key never leaves your device.
  • ParaSign signing vault (IndexedDB): if you sign documents, your post-quantum signing key (ML-DSA-65) is generated and kept in your browser’s IndexedDB, encrypted either with your passkey (WebAuthn-PRF → HKDF → AES-256-GCM) or, if your passkey provider cannot do that, with a passphrase (PBKDF2 → AES-256-GCM). The private key never leaves your device; only its public half is registered to your account.

You can delete all local data at any time: browser settings → paramant.app → Clear site data.

Servers & jurisdiction

All hosting and the encrypted file relay run on Hetzner infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany (EU/DE). Your account data, the relay, and the Certificate Transparency log are stored and processed in the EU only. We engage no hosting, storage, or compute provider outside the EU.

The only point at which a limited amount of data may reach a provider outside the EU is transactional email, depending on the provider chosen below; that single transfer, if it applies, is disclosed and safeguarded in the Subprocessors section. There is no other transfer of personal data outside the EU, and the file relay itself never leaves EU/GDPR jurisdiction.

Network edge

TLS is terminated by our own edge (Caddy), running on the same Hetzner infrastructure in Germany, with post-quantum key exchange. There is no third-party reverse proxy or CDN in front of the site: your connection reaches our server and nowhere else. Short-lived connection metadata (IP, timestamp) is processed transiently for security and abuse-prevention as described above, never for analytics.

Controller and processor roles

For the personal data of your PARAMANT account (your email, sign-in factors, plan, signing public key), PARAMANT is the controller and this policy governs that processing.

For the content of files you transfer or documents you sign, PARAMANT acts only as a processor on your behalf: you decide what to send and to whom, and you remain the controller of that content. Technically we cannot read it (it is encrypted client-side, RAM-only, burn-on-read), and we process it solely to deliver it. If you use PARAMANT in a professional context, and especially in sectors such as healthcare, legal, or government where files may contain special categories of data (Article 9 GDPR) or otherwise sensitive personal data, this processor relationship is set out in our Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which forms the Article 28 contract between you (controller) and PARAMANT (processor). PARAMANT does not determine the purposes of, or otherwise access, that content.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of them, email privacy@paramant.app; we respond within one month (Article 12(3)) and we do not charge for a reasonable request.

  • Access (Art. 15): obtain confirmation of, and a copy of, the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): have your data deleted, for example by closing your account, subject only to data we must keep by law (such as billing records).
  • Restriction (Art. 18): ask us to limit processing while a dispute or check is resolved.
  • Data portability (Art. 20): receive the account data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and have it sent to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Object (Art. 21): object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): where we rely on your consent, withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • No automated decisions (Art. 22): you are not subject to any decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling; we do none.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe we process your personal data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. You may also contact the supervisory authority in your own EU country of residence or work. We would, of course, appreciate the chance to address your concern first at privacy@paramant.app.

Children

PARAMANT is not directed at children under 16, the age of consent for information-society services in the Netherlands under the UAVG. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16; if you believe a minor has provided us data, contact privacy@paramant.app and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or the law. We will note any change via the "Last updated" date above, and for any material change affecting how we process your personal data we will notify account holders in advance (for example by email) and, where the change requires it, ask for your consent. Your rights under this policy are not reduced without notice.
