Use a passkey · Recommended

A passkey lives on this device and nowhere else. You unlock it with your fingerprint, your face or your device PIN, so there is nothing to remember and nothing to type. Best if you work mostly from one phone or one laptop.

Use an authenticator app

An app on your phone shows a fresh 6-digit code every 30 seconds and you type that code to sign in. Better if you move between several computers, or if colleagues share one workstation.

Which apps work?

Every standard authenticator app works, including Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator. For the strongest setup we recommend a SHA-256 app: Raivo or 2FAS (iPhone), Aegis (Android), 1Password, Bitwarden, Authy, or Ente Auth. Any of these is a great choice. More about authenticator apps.
