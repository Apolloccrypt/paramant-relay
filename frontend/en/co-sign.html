HOW THIS WORKS

Decryption and ML-DSA-65 signing happen locally. The relay receives ciphertext, your public key, the resulting signature and signed field coordinates, never document plaintext or your private key.

Post-quantum, zero-knowledge. Not eIDAS-qualified. A ParaSign signature is a Simple Electronic Signature (SES): an ML-DSA-65 cryptographic attestation, not a notarised legal signature under eIDAS or any qualified-trust regime (QES). Verify the document hash below against the file you received separately.
