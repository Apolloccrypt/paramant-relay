      Two products

      One for signing. One for sending.

      Both run on the same core, and both work in a plain browser. Nothing to install for you, nothing to install for the other side.

      • ParaSign

        Get a document signed.

        • Upload the PDF, place the fields, and mail it to whoever has to sign.
        • They sign with the link from that email. Signing an invitation needs no account.
        • You keep a receipt anyone can check offline, plus an entry in the public log.
        • A simple electronic signature under eIDAS. Not a qualified one, and we say so.
        What ParaSign isSign a document
      • ParaSend

        Send a file that disappears.

        • The file is encrypted in your browser before it leaves your device.
        • Our server only ever holds the sealed file in memory, never on a disk.
        • It is wiped after its last read, so there is nothing left to leak later.
        • That holds for the web app and the SDKs. The browser extensions still encrypt on our server, until that is changed.
        • In the web app you and the receiver are both online and compare a short code, and the file is handed over live. Sending to someone who is not online right now needs the API, the SDK, or the Send a link mode in the web app.

      How it works

      Three steps, and you are done.

      No plugin, no client software, no onboarding call. The whole thing happens in the tab you already have open.

      1. 01

        Pick the file

        Drop a PDF in your browser. It is encrypted on your own device, before anything is uploaded.

      2. 02

        Send the link

        The other side opens the link from the email and signs or reads it there. They do not have to sign up for anything.

      3. 03

        Keep the proof

        You get a receipt that can be checked without us, and a line in a public log that shows when it happened.
