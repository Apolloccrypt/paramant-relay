Frequently asked

I don't see the second email

Give it a minute — delivery can take up to 60 seconds. Check your spam / junk folder. The sender is noreply@paramant.app. Still nothing? Request a new setup link using the same email address.

Where's the 6-digit code in the email?

It isn't in the email. Codes are generated by an authenticator app on your phone after you scan the QR code in the setup email. Any standard app works, including Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and iCloud Keychain. For the strongest setup we recommend a SHA-256 app: Raivo (iOS) or Aegis (Android), both open source. Also great: 1Password, Bitwarden, Authy, Ente Auth, 2FAS. Full list and details.

I already completed the setup

Then you're done. Sign in with your email and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
