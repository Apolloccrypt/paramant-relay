Email verified

One step left. Open the second email.

Your address is confirmed and your account exists. Paramant has no passwords, so the last step is to link a passkey or an authenticator app, and that happens through the second mail we just sent you.

Do this now
Look for this subject line

The mail comes from noreply@paramant.app with the subject:

Complete your Paramant account setup

Open it and follow the setup link. There you choose a passkey (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello) or an authenticator app, and you save your backup codes. No app yet? Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator both work. Already using 1Password or Bitwarden? Keep it there. Any other standard authenticator works too, and the help page compares them. The 6-digit codes are produced by that app, never sent by email.

Frequently asked

I don't see the second email

The mail is on its way, but we cannot promise how fast your provider delivers it. Check your spam and junk folders; the sender is noreply@paramant.app. Still nothing after a few minutes? Ask for a new setup link with the same address.

Where's the 6-digit code in the email?

It is not in the mail, and it never will be. The codes are produced on your phone by the authenticator app, once you have scanned the QR code in the setup mail. Any standard app works; the help page compares them.

I already completed the setup

Then you are ready. Sign in with your email and either your passkey or the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
