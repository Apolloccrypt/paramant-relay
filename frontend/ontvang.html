What made this safe

Your browser made its own pair of keys when this page opened and kept the private half here, where it still is. The sender locked the file for the public half before it left their device, so what travelled was sealed for you and for nobody else (ML-KEM-768 with ECDH P-256 for the key exchange, AES-256-GCM for the file).

The short code you both read out was worked out on this side too, from your own keys. Two screens showing the same code is what tells you nobody swapped a key in between.

A transfer through this web app is read once. Each block was wiped on our side as your browser took it, and our copy is destroyed on its own clock as well if nobody ever comes for it. What our relay reported on the way is written below.
