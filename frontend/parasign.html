ParaSign

Post-quantum e-signing by Paramant

Sign PDFs in your browser with an ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204) signature. Your key never leaves your device, and every completed signature comes with a .psign receipt that anyone can verify offline, with or without the Paramant relay.

Sign a document → See pricing →
01 / What it does

Sign, co-sign, prove

ParaSign is the signing half of Paramant. It runs on the same post-quantum core as ParaSend, and the document you sign stays private: the plaintext and the private key never leave your browser.

PDF editor and visible seal

Place text, dates and annotations, manage pages, then drop the signature stamp on any page. The stamp is what readers see; the .psign envelope is the proof.

Co-signing with routing order

Add each person who needs to sign. Every invitation is bound to the exact email address you enter, and the encrypted document travels with the request.

Any file type, by hash

Files that are not PDFs get a hash-only attestation: you sign the SHA3-256 of the bytes and recipients verify by re-hashing the same file.

Public transparency log

Every signature is entered into a public, append-only log. Inspect it on the CT Log.

Offline verification

A signed document verifies without contacting us. Check one on the verify page: no account, no API key.

Developer API from Pro

Plug signing into your own apps and quotes with the developer API and webhooks. See the documentation.

03 / How to begin

Three steps to a signed document

Signing a document needs a Paramant account, because the signing key is bound to it. Opening a signing request someone sent you does not: invited signers follow the personal link in their email.

  1. Create a free account. No card, no time limit. The free tier covers 2 signatures a month.
  2. Open ParaSign and pick your document. PDFs get a visible signature stamp you place on any page; other file types are attested by hash. Add co-signers if you need them, then sign. Your key is unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or a security key the moment you sign, and it is never exported.
  3. Download and share. You get the stamped PDF and the .psign envelope. Anyone can check them on the verify page, offline, without an account.
04 / Why you can trust it

Every claim has a place to check it

You should not have to trust us. You should be able to check. Each point below links to where you can.

Post-quantum signatures

ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204), generated in your browser. The private key never reaches the relay. The algorithm register on the crypto agility page lists it as the loaded default.

You own your keys

Generated on your device, never sent. We see only the public half. How that works is on the security page.

Verifiable, not trust-me

A public CT log and receipts anyone can check offline. Open the CT Log or the verify page.

Source-available, EU jurisdiction

The relay is source-available on GitHub, and runs under EU jurisdiction. Who is behind it is on the about page.

Post-quantum, zero-knowledge. Not eIDAS-qualified. A ParaSign signature is a Simple Electronic Signature (SES): an ML-DSA-65 cryptographic attestation produced in your browser. It is not a notarised legal signature under eIDAS or any qualified-trust regime (QES). We state exactly what it is and what it is not.
